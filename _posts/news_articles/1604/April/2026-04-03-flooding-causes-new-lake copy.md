**MAPLEWOOD***- Last month, the senior authority figures of Maplewood, located in the Southern Freelands, attended an all-hands meeting behind closed doors. No public statement has been issued, no meeting notes have been released, and officials reached for comment have declined to provide one.

The meeting occurred during the same period in which SIP's correspondents confirmed increased Veilwalker activity across multiple nations, and world governments have begun organizing their responses. Whether Maplewood's leadership convened to address this threat, or for unrelated reasons, has not been established.

What can be established is that a decision was made to hold this meeting, and a separate decision was made to not to inform the public of its contents, by the same people.

Special Investigative Press will continue to pursue this story, and will release a statement as soon as it is made.
