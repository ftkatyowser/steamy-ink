### Out of Game

For those who are not aware, Poppy is taking a sabbatical. The presses, however, run whether or not she is present! I'm still writing and running the paper behind the scenes.

Enzo is acting Editor-in-Chief so players have someone to go to in-game. Out-of-game, continue to send articles and tips to me. Or you can send them to Ryan anyway, I'm not a cop, I can't tell you what to do.

Expect some shenanigans from Steamy Ink as he becomes drunk with editing power.

Special Investigative Press will stay the reliable source of news, although you may see fewer issues in Maplewood as the apocalypse winds down.
