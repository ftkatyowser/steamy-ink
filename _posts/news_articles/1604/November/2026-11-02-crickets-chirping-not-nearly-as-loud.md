With all the excitement with the Veilwalkers it is important that someone keeps track of just how noisy the Crickets are. As we all know there is a correlation between the noise of the Crickets and Sky Beast attacks. Perhaps it is the Veilwalkers consuming all the crickets, or maybe its just the fact that it is November. Who knows? Not me. But, what counts is that with the diminished Cricket activity there have also been nearly no Sky Beast sightings. Are we free of the sky beasts? Unlikely, they will strike again some day when we least expect it. But what you can expect is that we no longer will have to fear Schmimbri's terrible sauce ruining our dinners. The sauce that's so mediocre even Emerick Barnes doesn't think he could convince anyone to buy it. Several accounts even mention how it could cause you to become enraged like the time Catori shot me for eating her last scotch egg. Thankfully, this was a time before Schmimbri was ruining food. A time we'll return to again.
