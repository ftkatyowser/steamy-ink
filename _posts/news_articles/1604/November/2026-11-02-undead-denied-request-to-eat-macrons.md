The accursed undead of Maplewood were out in powerful droves last month! They terrorized our community, and even disrupted Tabeo's dessert tasting competition, held in the Vargainen Embassy. The flavors were heard to be some rather eccentric tastes such as: "raspberry-lime-cayenne", "mocha-lemon", "maple-blueberry-bacon", and "cinnamon-cream cheese-peach". But the most eccentric taste of the competition was its uninvited guests.

The meeting was interrupted several times, first by an alleged member of a succubus's harem, demanding Imbri return her spark. Some say this resulted in Imbri's and Liotru's own sparks leaving their bodies. What are those two doing with her spark in the first place!?

After a resolution, the party grew wise and the doors to the embassy were sealed by magic. No more interruption, the brave taste-testers thought.

Alas, optimism could not stop the restless ghouls wandering Maplewood from their attempts to horrify. A roaming wraith terrorized the windows of the embassy before moving on. Accounts from the nearby Bazaar claim they even saw a snow goblin menacingly approach the embassy, followed by an uproarious chorus of "Noooo!" which could be heard from outside.

Fearsome fights with patrolling bodaks were ongoing for those outside of the embassy, while inside the desserts were blissfully consumed. When asked if their guardsman, the merc known as Flower, saw anything, he reportedly looked out the window and declared, "It's dark, I can't see anything out there."

Dark, indeed! At the resolution of the competition (which was championed by none other than Rex) the necromancer assaulting the embassy all evening rushed in to wreak havoc as the lock was lifted. As one first-hand account claims, "He was buff, in more ways than one" but his tenacity stood no chance! While he felled a few flat-footed adventurers and hacked off a few limbs, in the end, they put him to rest. Gooood riddance!

Congratulations to Rex for his keen senses.
