It isn't just hot sauce that Schimbri has ruined. To avoid association with his mediocre product and to promote bringing something actually good into the world local schools have begun calling themselves "ools". We interviewed one local teacher about this new change and they had this to say: "What are you talking about? That's the stupidest thing I've ever heard. Go away."

As you can see Schimbri is the stupidest thing they've ever heard and they want him to go away. They don't even want to know his name. Ools everywhere have begun asking their patrons if further changes are in order. We attempted to contact the Scholar to see if they are considering rebranding to the Olar but they were not available for comment. The only reasonable conclusion is that the Sept is in deep deliberation over just how urgently they need to make this change. It is uncertain if the gods eat or not, but if they do surely they would not want to fear Schimbri's terrible sauce ruining their dinners. The sauce that's so mediocre even Emerick Barnes doesn't think he could convince anyone to buy it. Several accounts even mention how it could cause you to become enraged like the time Catori shot me for eating her last scotch egg. Thankfully, this was a time before Schimbri was ruining food. A time we'll return to again.
