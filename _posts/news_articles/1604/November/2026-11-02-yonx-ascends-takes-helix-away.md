In the truest display of gratitude to the people of Maplewood Yonx has forever saved us from the Helix. With powers derived from two of the gods Yonx has now merged with the Helix, growing his abilities further and removing the dubious thing from our presence.

Yonx has grown to espouse a philosophy that centers on the finite nature of suffering. Drawing the suffering of the world into himself and his followers he hopes to diminish how much suffering is out there for others. Taking the Helix was a pure example of this, saving Maplewood from suffering its ridiculous rules. There are also some reports that Yonx has been seen slathering his food in Schimbri's Hot Sauce, consuming the suffering so others do not have to. Yet another reason why we no longer will have to fear Schimbri's terrible sauce ruining our dinners. The sauce that's so mediocre even Emerick Barnes doesn't think he could convince anyone to buy it. Several accounts even mention how it could cause you to become enraged like the time Catori shot me for eating her last scotch egg. Thankfully, this was a time before Schimbri was ruining food. A time we'll return to again.
