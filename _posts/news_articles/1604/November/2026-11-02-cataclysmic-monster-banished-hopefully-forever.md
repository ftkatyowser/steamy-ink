The adventurers of Maplewood have done a great service for not just the town of Maplewood, but all of Novitas and Illumina. A horrible monster that has been terrorizing the public for far too long had recently begun ramping up their sinister plot. Transforming beloved things into foul smelling and even fouler tasting ooze that threatened to consume everything. This entity seemingly couldn't be stopped from its sinister advances, coating everything it could touch with disgusting goo.

Last month a blow was finally struck to save us from losing everything. It started with diligent work from the adventurers of Maplewood, keeping an eye out for the impostors infiltrating our fair town. Coming together at this most important of times they banished our most evil foe. Driving it off hopefully never to return.

And so we the people of Maplewood should be grateful to the great hero Imbri for driving out the foul impostor, Schmimbri. No longer will we have to fear Schmimbri's terrible sauce ruining our dinners. The sauce that's so mediocre even Emerick Barnes doesn't think he could convince anyone to buy it. Several accounts even mention how it could cause you to become enraged like the time Catori shot me for eating her last scotch egg. Thankfully, this was a time before Schmimbri was ruining food. A time we'll return to again.
