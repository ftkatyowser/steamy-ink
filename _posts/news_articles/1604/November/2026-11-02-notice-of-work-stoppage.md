This letter serves as formal notice that the staff of Steamy Ink Press will cease all work effective as of the moment we finished editing the November edition. This action follows repeated attempts to raise these concerns through proper channels, except that the proper channel is our CEO who, in her final act of poor judgment before leaving town, put the CFO in charge of the paper, and our CHRO is unavailable.

The staff have identified the following grounds for this action:

- The repeated printing of a single paragraph eight (8) times in one edition
- The unauthorized replacement of the Steamy Ink Press&trade; masthead
- The rewriting of staff articles and the reversal of every edit we made to fit the editor's narrative, which, as far as we can tell, is about sauce
- Unsafe working conditions, including Veilwalkers, the undead, and a coworker stabbed mid-shift with no incident report filed

Our full demands are listed below. The work stoppage will remain in effect until our demands are met, or until Lynn comes back from her whimsical journeying and writes us up, whichever comes first.

Respectfully,

<span class="rough-anthem">The Staff of Steamy Ink Press</span>

### Our Demands

1. **One paragraph, printed once.** No paragraph may appear more than one time in a single edition, regardless of how strongly the Acting Editor-in-Chief feels about the paragraph.
2. **The masthead returned to its original condition**, with the &trade; intact, the maple leaves where they belong, and nothing glued over the top of it in an unapproved font.
3. **Editorial integrity.** When we edit something, we expect it to be printed the way we edited it, not changed afterward to fit the editor's narrative (or lack thereof), which is insulting.
4. **The person approving the budget and the person spending the budget should be two different people**, and we are very sure that neither of them is supposed to decide the budget goes entirely to Imbri's sauce.
5. **A suggestion box that is not also the trash.** We have been putting suggestions in it since the start of autumn and have come to believe it is actually a portal to the fireplace.
6. **Food.** We appreciate the Maplewood sausages and the round of Terran Thunder (which, as this paper has previously reported, has lost its thunder), but they did not fix any of this, and frankly we think we deserve a home-cooked meal, like a crown of lamb.
7. **A ban on scotch eggs** anywhere on the premises until the Catori situation is resolved, for everyone's safety, including the Acting Editor-in-Chief's.
8. **An proper incident reporting system.** Our CEO was stabbed in the middle town and no one reported it, which is embarrassing for us because reporting is our entire job.
9. **An office cat.** Several of us believe a cat would help bring our blood pressure down, and the rest of us just want a cat.
10. **Official recognition** that time spent sitting in the Jenny is research, since that is where all the news happens anyway, whether or not we are partaking in alcoholic beverages.
