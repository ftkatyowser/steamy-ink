<span class="rough-anthem">Dearest Reader,</span>

As the days grow shorter and the nights grow colder, you might think that the residents of Maplewood would be preparing themselves to hunker down for another long winter, but this author is here to confirm that the town is, as always, buzzing with chatter about the latest intrigues. Lucky for you, she has toiled to gather all this tea-table talk and continues her commitment to keep the people of Maplewood up to date on the things that really matter. To those naysayers who claim she has no right to "spread gossip," this author would like to remind them of the age old saying: Where there is smoke, there is fire. And where there is fire, there are dragons. Or drakes, to be more precise.

SIP was sent an anonymous tip about a new addition to Maplewood's most inseparable pairs, as overheard in town. Who could possibly rival the potent connection between Cordon and Makhno? The unseverable bond between Micah and Audra? Having finally learned the identities of the would-be lovers, this author was shocked to discover that the infamous new couple was none other than Lapis and SIP's very own Chief Executive Officer, Poppy Marianus! What follows are nothing but the undeniable facts of the incident that lead to the uncovering of their forbidden tryst:

On a Friday evening in early October, Lapis and Poppy were seen walking out into the deep woods alone, allegedly to the former's shrine, though no one was around to verify if that was truly their destination. After a period of time that was much too long to be considered appropriate for a married woman to be in the woods with a "friend," the pair came back to town, and were nearly inseparable the entire weekend. One reputable source reported hearing Mrs. Marianus admit that she even has a "key to Lapis' bedroom." While this could be a product of Poppy's particular brand of humor, this author suspects the comment is more than just a simple joke.

The very next morning, right after the premier of her newest work for the Maplewood Actors Guild, Lapis was seen approaching Poppy from behind and proceeded to stab her, immediately fleeing the scene by leyline with her unconscious body. Despite this happening right in front of her husband, the Consul seemed unbothered witnessing the attack and subsequent kidnapping of his wife. Many saw Consul Marianus huff away in the direction of Lapis' shrine, and though he (and his bruised pride) returned later that day, no one can speak to what happened or if he encountered Poppy and Lapis at all. All we know for sure is that the possible paramours haven't been seen since.

Several theories on how this moon-crossed romance turned so ugly have circulated, though none are confirmed. To this author, the cause is clear: this can be nothing but a crime of passion. What reason but jealousy would Lapis have to commit such an act right in front of Titus? Why did he wait so politely for Poppy's play to finish before carrying her off if not out of consideration for his beloved? Regarding Mrs. Marianus' own motivations in entertaining Lapis' romantic advances, it seems obvious that her infamous ambition for upward social mobility simply couldn't resist the chance at an even loftier title than her current husband can give her. Apparently, the thought of marrying into a noble bloodline was too attractive an offer to pass up. Many eye-witness accounts claim that Poppy was seen both chiding and doting upon the scaled scion, an attitude suspiciously similar to that of an attentive and devoted spouse. Was Poppy practicing for a new set of wifely duties? Even more suspicious, it's been said that after the Katullin family held a "gathering of concern" regarding Poppy's health back in the spring, she vanished, only to be found hiding in Lapis' shrine. Perhaps an early sign of their amour? Regardless, Titus should be wary about whatever comes next, as Poppy's marriage dagger and friendship with Lady Evilynn are well-documented, and a homicide or a divorce could be right around the corner for the Consul if he isn't careful.

So, dear reader, this author leaves it up to you to sort through the facts and decide for yourselves if this entanglement is only a passing flirtation or something more. Either way, you can count on us to report any further developments on this sensational scandal as soon as we hear it.

<span class="rough-anthem">Yours Truly,<br>Madam Mapleleaf</span>
