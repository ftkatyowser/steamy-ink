A certain paper described last month's events as "confusing" and "completely unclear," which is news to those of us who were there and paying attention.

The Avatar of the Craftsman built an apparatus to block the Entity from communicating with the Veilwalkers. The pieces had to be built to the Avatar's exact instructions, and the adventurers of Maplewood had to complete a series of tasks to power it, which they did, while some people were apparently not listening.

When activated, Veilwalkers immediately were rendered confused almost mindless. They're still out there, and they're still dangerous, but without the Entity they can't coordinate and they don't do well functioning on their own.

The Entity was a creation of Nox, which is how Nox knew how to use Blight for it. Draconus' ordeals paid for the magic needed to cure the infection, and the Mother is taking some suffering onto herself to cure those already infected with the Veilwalker Curse and keep anyone else from catching it.

Veilwalker numbers are expected to drop from here. Various Avatars explained all of this throughout the building process.
