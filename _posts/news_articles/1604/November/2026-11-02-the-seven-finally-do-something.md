In an event people are calling "confusing" and "completely unclear" the Avatar of the Craftsman has built "an apparatus" that once activated did something. No one is entirely sure what that something is. The Avatar tried to explain, but I wasn't really listening. If only the Avatar were a better communicator. As a result of this apparatus we are now safe from the Veilwalkers. Sure they might attack us, and try to kill us, and eat all our potted plants, and our herb gardens, and maybe our neighbor's dog, but we're safe. It maybe wasn't the most effective defeat of the Veilwalkers, but the Entity is probably really upset right now. According to a report from one drunken celebrant at the Spinning Jenny "We sure told them. Finally got those filthy squirrels out of our town." When asked what that had to do with the Veilwalkers they replied "Everything, don't you know the Squirrels are their spies, watching your every move." They weren't available for further followup, but if they had been available they would have likely added that this was a great day, especially because we no longer have to fear Schmimbri's terrible sauce ruining our dinners. The sauce that's so mediocre even Emerick Barnes doesn't think he could convince anyone to buy it. Several accounts even mention how it could cause you to become enraged like the time Catori shot me for eating her last scotch egg. Thankfully, this was a time before Schmimbri was ruining food. A time we'll return to again.
