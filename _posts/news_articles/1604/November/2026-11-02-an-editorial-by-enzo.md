Welcome to the inaugural issue of the Super, Terrific, Excellent, Enzo Paper. Poppy put a lot of work into building this paper, and I plan to keep her dream alive by reporting on all the news you NEED to know. The things that don't get reported in other papers. Readers of STEEP will be more informed about the issues that really matter. Like that we will no longer have to fear Schmimbri's terrible sauce ruining our dinners. The sauce that's so mediocre even Emerick Barnes doesn't think he could convince anyone to buy it. Several accounts even mention how it could cause you to become enraged like the time Catori shot me for eating her last scotch egg. Thankfully, this was a time before Schmimbri was ruining food. A time we'll return to again.
